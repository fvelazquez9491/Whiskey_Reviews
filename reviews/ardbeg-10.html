Islay Single Malt Hebrides, Scotland 93 / 100

Ardbeg 10 Year Old

Universally revered by peat aficionados as one of the peatiest, smokiest malts on Earth. What sets Ardbeg 10 apart is its remarkable paradox: intense maritime smoke harnessed to zesty lemon, black pepper, and creamy sweetness.

Distillery Ardbeg Distillery
Region Islay, Scotland
ABV 46% Non-Chill Filtered
Peat Level 50-55 PPM Heavy Peat
Ardbeg 10 Year Old poured neat in an amber tasting lounge
Sensory Analysis

Official Tasting Notes

Evaluated neat at room temperature in a Glencairn glass.

Color

Appearance in the Glass
Pale Straw & Light Sunlight Gold

In the glass, Ardbeg 10 is deceptively pale—a light straw and sparkling sunlight gold. Because it is non-chill filtered and naturally colored, a drop of water produces a lovely hazy scotch mist. Rapid, energetic tears coat the glass wall.

Pale Straw Natural Color Energetic Legs

Smell

Aromatic Bouquet
Smoky Campfire, Tangy Lemon & Smoked Bacon

A glorious explosion of turf peat smoke, fresh lime zest, black pepper, and maritime salinity. As the dram opens, notes of crispy smoked bacon, eucalyptus, warm asphalt tar, and roasted espresso beans emerge with thrilling intensity.

Campfire Peat Fresh Lime Zest Crispy Bacon Espresso Bean

Taste

Flavors & Finish
Crackling Peat, Citrus Tang, Licorice & Tarry Finish

The entry starts with surprisingly sweet malt and creamy vanilla before crackling peat fire engulfs the palate. Tangy lemon juice, black pepper, black licorice, and salted caramel burst forth. The finish is remarkably long, smoky, tarry, and dry.

Crackling Peat Salted Caramel Black Licorice Endless Smoke

The Peat Paradox & Tasting Narrative

Ardbeg distillery sits on the rocky south coast of Islay and has weathered numerous closures throughout the 20th century before being triumphantly revived in 1997. The secret behind Ardbeg’s legendary flavor profile is its unique distillation apparatus: a purifier on the spirit still that allows only the lightest, most floral vapors to condense into the final cut.

This technical marvel produces what enthusiasts call "The Peaty Paradox." While the malt is heavily peated up to 55 PPM (parts per million of phenols), the underlying spirit remains bright, citrusy, and floral, creating a thrilling high-wire act of contrasting flavors in every sip.

Tasting Room Verdict

"Electrifying and uncompromising. An essential dram for anyone seeking the pinnacle of phenolic power balanced by pristine citrus acidity."
