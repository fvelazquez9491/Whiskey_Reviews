Single Pot Still Irish County Cork, Ireland 94 / 100

Redbreast 12 Year Old

The crown jewel of traditional Irish pot still whiskey. Distilled from a mash of malted and unmalted barley in traditional copper pot stills, then matured in a combination of bourbon barrels and Oloroso sherry butts.

Distillery Midleton Distillery
Region County Cork, Ireland
ABV 40% (80 Proof)
Style Single Pot Still
Redbreast 12 Year Old showcasing its warm golden radiance in fine glassware
Sensory Analysis

Official Tasting Notes

Tasted neat at room temperature in a Glencairn glass.

Color

Appearance in the Glass
Bright Warm Gold & Burnished Brass

In the glass, Redbreast 12 exhibits an enticing warm gold with polished brass highlights. Swirling reveals a rich, clingy sheet of spirit that breaks into persistent, rounded droplets with excellent viscosity.

Aromatic Bouquet
Orchard Fruits, Roasted Hazelnuts & Pot Still Spice

Wonderfully complex and aromatic. Succulent ripe peach, baked pear, and blackcurrant jam combine with roasted hazelnuts, marzipan, toasted oak, and the unmistakable spicy, prickling tingle of raw unmalted grain.

Baked Pear Roasted Hazelnut Marzipan Pot Still Spice

Taste

Flavors & Finish
Velvety Creaminess, Sherry Sweetness & Warming Ginger

Luxuriously silky and mouth-coating. Flavors unfold with dried sultanas, creamy vanilla flan, and orange zest, followed by a burst of warming pot still spice—ginger, cardamom, and white pepper. The finish is long, creamy, and gently spiced.

Velvet Texture Oloroso Sherry Candied Ginger Creamy Finish

The Irish Heritage & Tasting Narrative

Single Pot Still whiskey is unique to Ireland and represents the historical heartbeat of Irish distillation. Born in the 18th century as an ingenious response to a British tax on malted barley, distillers began mixing unmalted green barley with malted grain. The resulting spirit possessed an extraordinary creamy, oily mouthfeel and an unmistakable spicy character that defined Irish whiskey's golden age.

Redbreast 12 is affectionately known as "The Irish Robin" and has kept the single pot still tradition alive when many other distilleries converted to light grain blends. Maturation in Spanish Oloroso sherry butts gives it a rich depth that balances the zesty pot still grain spice with perfection.

Tasting Room Verdict

"An absolute masterclass in texture. If you have only tasted blended Irish whiskies, Redbreast 12 will fundamentally revolutionize your appreciation of Irish distilling."
