Japanese Single Malt Osaka, Japan 95 / 100

Yamazaki 12 Year Old

The flagship single malt from Japan’s first commercial whisky distillery, founded by Shinjiro Torii in 1923. Matured in American oak, Spanish sherry casks, and revered indigenous Japanese Mizunara oak.

Distillery Yamazaki (Suntory)
Region Shimamoto, Osaka
ABV 43% (86 Proof)
Wood Type Mizunara, American & Sherry
Yamazaki 12 Year Old poured neat in minimalist tasting glassware
Sensory Analysis

Official Tasting Notes

Tasted neat at room temperature with five minutes of gentle aeration.

Color

Appearance in the Glass
Pure Luminous Gold & Pale Topaz

Pours a brilliant, crystal-clear pure gold with pale topaz and honey shimmer. In the glass, it possesses a graceful, medium weight with delicate, finely spaced tears that roll gently down the walls.

Pure Gold Pale Topaz Delicate Legs

Smell

Aromatic Bouquet
Ripe Peach, Candied Orange & Mizunara Sandalwood

Exquisitely floral, delicate, and layered. Fragrant aromas of ripe white peach, persimmon, and pineapple blossom into vanilla cream, clove, and the intoxicating, temple-like incense of Japanese Mizunara oak.

White Peach Persimmon Mizunara Incense Sandalwood

Taste

Flavors & Finish
Silky Tropical Fruit, Coconut, Cinnamon & Serene Finish

Smooth, silky entry with red currant, toasted coconut, buttered toast, and poached pear. Mid-palate yields gentle spice and cedar wood. The finish is serene, prolonged, and clean with lingering ginger and fragrant wood smoke.

Toasted Coconut Poached Pear Gentle Cedar Serene Finish

The Yamazaki Philosophy & Tasting Narrative

Surrounded by bamboo groves and mist-shrouded hills at the confluence of three pure rivers (Katsura, Uji, and Kizu), the Yamazaki distillery enjoys high humidity and pristine soft water ideal for maturation. The late master blender Keizo Saji emphasized harmony above all: no single flavor is allowed to overwhelm another.

The secret weapon of Yamazaki 12 is Japanese Mizunara oak (*Quercus crispula*). Highly porous and notoriously difficult for coopers to work with, Mizunara requires years of interaction with spirit before it releases its ethereal, aromatic compounds reminiscent of temple incense, agarwood, and dried citrus.

Tasting Room Verdict

"An extraordinary study in elegance and precision. Yamazaki 12 displays a level of refinement, nuance, and aromatic beauty rarely matched in the whisky world."
