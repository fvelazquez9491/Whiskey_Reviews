Kentucky Straight Rye USA 92 / 100

Michter's US*1 Single Barrel Rye

A supreme exemplar of the Kentucky rye style. Handcrafted in toasted, fire-charred new American white oak barrels, bottled from individual single barrels with painstaking attention to flavor richness over high yield.

Distillery Michter's Distillery
Region Louisville, Kentucky
ABV 42.4% (84.8 Proof)
Format Single Barrel Selection
Michter's US*1 Single Barrel Rye neat pour showing deep chestnut clarity
Sensory Analysis

Official Tasting Notes

Tasted neat in a tasting glass at room temperature.

Color

Appearance in the Glass
Deep Copper-Bronze & Brilliant Chestnut

In the glass, Michter's Rye glows with a deep copper-bronze and radiant chestnut hue. Swirling reveals full-bodied, thick legs that form deliberate crowns on the glass, characteristic of Michter's low barrel entry proof.

Deep Copper Chestnut Hue Patient Legs

Smell

Aromatic Bouquet
Candied Citrus, Dark Cherry & Baking Spices

Wonderfully warm and inviting. Waves of candied orange peel, brandied dark cherries, and pungent freshly cracked rye grain mingle with cinnamon stick, cracked clove, sweet butterscotch, and warm vanilla bean.

Candied Orange Dark Cherries Rye Grain Clove & Cinnamon

Taste

Flavors & Finish
Rich Dark Chocolate, Pepper, Honey & Clean Warm Finish

The palate is rich, spicy, and satisfyingly thick. Flavors of dark chocolate, baked red apples, and cracked black pepper expand into sweet toffee and orange marmalade. The finish is remarkably warm, clean, and gently lingering with oak spices.

Dark Chocolate Baked Apple Orange Marmalade Clean Warm Finish

The Michter's Method & Tasting Narrative

Michter’s traces its roots back to America’s first distilling company, established in 1753 in Schaefferstown, Pennsylvania. Today distilled in Louisville, Kentucky, Michter’s adheres to uncompromising, costly production standards: they air-dry their oak staves for up to 36 months, toast the barrels before charring, and enter the whiskey into barrels at a historically low 103 proof.

This low barrel entry proof allows the whiskey to extract high concentrations of wood sugars early in the aging process. As a result, Michter’s US*1 Rye avoids the grassy, aggressive burn of young ryes, offering instead a luxurious dessert-like richness elevated by crisp rye spice.

Tasting Room Verdict

"One of the friendliest, most luxurious rye whiskies on the American market. Extraordinary both neat and as the foundation of an unforgettable Manhattan."
