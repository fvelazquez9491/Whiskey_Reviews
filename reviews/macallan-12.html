Speyside Single Malt Scotland 91 / 100

The Macallan 12 Year Old Double Cask

A masterful marriage of hand-selected American and European Oloroso sherry seasoned oak casks, The Macallan 12 Double Cask demonstrates how warm American oak vanilla complements European spice and dried fruit.

Distillery The Macallan
Region Speyside, Scotland
ABV 43% (86 Proof)
Cask Maturation Sherry Oak (US & EU)
The Macallan 12 Double Cask neat pour in classic crystal glassware
Sensory Analysis

Official Tasting Notes

Detailed evaluation captured neat in a Glencairn tasting glass at 18°C.

Color

Appearance in the Glass
Harvest Sun & Warm Amber Gold

In the glass, it gleams with a radiant harvest sun tint—completely natural with no caramel coloring. As swirled, it coats the contours of the Glencairn glass with generous, slow-moving tears that reveal a silky, moderate-to-full viscosity.

Warm Gold Amber Glints Dense Legs

Smell

Aromatic Bouquet
Creamy Butterscotch, Citrus & Candied Ginger

The nose opens immediately with rich butterscotch pudding and baked toffee apples. Gentle aeration uncovers candied orange peel, warm vanilla custard, fresh oak shavings, and a delicate dusting of nutmeg and ground ginger from the European sherry wood.

Butterscotch Toffee Apple Candied Orange Vanilla Bean

Taste

Flavors & Finish
Honeyed Raisins, Sweet Oak & Lingering Spice

The palate is soft, coating, and exquisitely balanced. Creamy honeycomb and plump sultanas meet gentle baking spices, wood tannin, and citrus zest. The finish lingers warmly with sweet sherry dried fruit, roasted almonds, and a dry, comforting oak resonance.

Honeycomb Sultanas Cinnamon Long Finish

The Tasting Experience & In-Depth Review

The Macallan’s Double Cask range was crafted to marry the rich, unctuous influence of traditional European oak with the sweeter, approachable vanilla character imparted by American oak. Both oak types are seasoned with dry Oloroso sherry in Jerez, Spain, for approximately 18 months before making their way north to Speyside.

What makes the 12 Year Old Double Cask particularly captivating is its equilibrium. Where older Macallan expressions can lean heavily into rich Christmas cake and dark cocoa, this 12-year expression maintains a vibrant, sunny personality. The American oak brings an almost confectionary warmth—marshmallow, coconut blossom, and creamy butterscotch—which prevents the spicy European oak tannins from ever feeling astringent.

Tasting Room Verdict

"An exemplary introduction to sherry-aged Speyside malt. Approachable enough for beginners to savor neat, yet complex enough for seasoned enthusiasts to appreciate the nuanced dual-oak maturation."
