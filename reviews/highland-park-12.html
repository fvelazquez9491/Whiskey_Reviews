Islands Single Malt Orkney, Scotland 91 / 100

Highland Park 12 "Viking Honour"

Distilled on the remote, windswept Orkney Islands off Scotland's northern coast since 1798. Highland Park balances aromatic heather peat smoke from Hobbister Moor with rich sherry seasoned European oak casks.

Distillery Highland Park
Region Kirkwall, Orkney
ABV 40% (80 Proof)
Cask Maturation Sherry Seasoned Oak
Highland Park 12 Viking Honour showcasing its glowing natural amber depth
Sensory Analysis

Official Tasting Notes

Evaluated neat at room temperature in a Glencairn glass.

Color

Appearance in the Glass
Glowing Natural Amber & Golden Topaz

Pours a glowing, completely natural amber with warm golden topaz highlights. As it coats the glass, it produces medium-weight, persistent legs that slide evenly down the side of the glass.

Natural Amber Golden Topaz Even Legs

Smell

Aromatic Bouquet
Heather Honey, Soft Smoke & Winter Fruitcake

Distinctive and evocative. A sweet halo of Orkney heather honey leads the nose, intertwined with delicate, aromatic floral peat smoke. Layers of Seville orange marmalade, rich Christmas fruitcake, and dried apricot follow seamlessly.

Heather Honey Floral Peat Orange Marmalade Fruitcake

Taste

Flavors & Finish
Malted Honey, Dried Berries & Lingering Wood Smoke

Full and rounded on the palate. Honeyed malt and dark orchard berries meld with warm cinnamon and soft, aromatic wood smoke. The finish is gently dry, comforting, and long, lingering with sweet heather embers and spicy oak.

Honeyed Malt Dark Berries Cinnamon Heather Embers

The Viking Heritage & Tasting Narrative

Located at 59 degrees north latitude, Orkney has virtually no trees due to ferocious Atlantic winds. The peat harvested from nearby Hobbister Moor is composed entirely of heather that has decomposed over 4,000 years, rather than moss or wood. When burned in the kiln, this unique peat yields a floral, aromatic smoke distinctly different from the medicinal, iodine smoke of Islay.

Highland Park’s commitment to hand-turned floor maltings and sherry seasoned oak casks creates a multi-dimensional whisky that bridges smoky and unpeated styles. It serves as the ultimate transitional dram for Scotch enthusiasts curious about exploring peated whiskies.

Tasting Room Verdict

"The consummate all-rounder. Highland Park 12 delivers an exquisite harmony of gentle floral peat, luscious sherry fruit, and heather honey sweetness."
