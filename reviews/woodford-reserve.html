Kentucky Straight Bourbon USA 90 / 100

Woodford Reserve Distiller's Select

Distilled on historic copper pot stills among the bluegrass pastures of Woodford County, Kentucky. This quintessential bourbon balances over 200 distinct flavor notes, seamlessly weaving caramel, rich vanilla, cocoa, and baking spice.

Distillery Woodford Reserve
Region Versailles, Kentucky
ABV 45.2% (90.4 Proof)
Mash Bill 72% Corn, 18% Rye, 10% Malt
Woodford Reserve Distiller's Select bottle and tasting dram in warm ambient light
Sensory Analysis

Official Tasting Notes

Tasted neat in a tasting glass at room temperature.

Color

Appearance in the Glass
Clean Brilliant Amber & Gleaming Copper

In the glass, Woodford presents a sparkling, clear honey amber with shimmering copper reflections. When swirled, it displays medium-plus viscosity, with elegant tears that bead slowly along the rim before descending in fine threads.

Brilliant Amber Copper Gleam Graceful Legs

Smell

Aromatic Bouquet
Rich Dried Fruit, Vanilla Bean & Toasted Brioche

Warm and inviting aromatics greet the nose immediately. Generous dried apricots, golden raisins, and candied orange peel merge with rich vanilla bean paste, toasted oak, a dusting of dark cocoa powder, and a faint hint of garden mint.

Vanilla Bean Dried Apricot Dark Cocoa Sweet Brioche

Taste

Flavors & Finish
Rich Caramel, Rye Spice, Espresso & Silky Warmth

Round, complex, and gently chewy on the palate. Flavors of toffee butterscotch and roasted pecans arrive first, accented by peppery rye spice, cinnamon stick, and espresso crema. The finish is silky, clean, satisfyingly warm, and deeply comforting.

Buttery Toffee Toasted Pecan Cinnamon Stick Silky Finish

The Bluegrass Tradition & Tasting Narrative

The site of the Woodford Reserve Distillery is among Kentucky's oldest and most venerated distillation locations, with spirits crafted on this ground since 1812. Woodford Reserve is unusual in the bourbon industry for utilizing genuine copper pot stills alongside column stills, and for using stone barrel warehouses that cycle through temperatures to accelerate gentle oak extraction.

Bottled at a sturdy 90.4 proof (45.2% ABV), it hits the exact sweet spot where ethanol heat does not overpower subtle esters. It stands as both a cornerstone of classic cocktails—like the Old Fashioned and Manhattan—and an exceptionally rewarding sipper on its own.

Tasting Room Verdict

"The quintessential benchmark of premium Kentucky bourbon. Impeccably balanced between sweet vanilla corn sweetness and lively rye spice."
