Speyside Single Malt Scotland 92 / 100

The Balvenie 14 Year Old Caribbean Cask

A delicious demonstration of cask finishing mastery by Malt Master David C. Stewart MBE. Fourteen years in traditional American oak casks followed by finishing in barrels that previously held select West Indian rum.

Distillery The Balvenie
Region Dufftown, Speyside
ABV 43% (86 Proof)
Cask Finish West Indian Rum Casks
The Balvenie 14 Caribbean Cask glowing with warm honey-amber hues
Sensory Analysis

Official Tasting Notes

Evaluated neat at room temperature in a Glencairn glass.

Color

Appearance in the Glass
Rich Golden Amber & Honeyed Bronze

In the glass, Caribbean Cask shines with a rich golden amber hue reminiscent of dark clover honey. It displays medium viscosity, clinging pleasantly to the glass walls and forming steady, relaxed legs that suggest sweet residual esters.

Honey Gold Bronze Sheen Steady Legs

Smell

Aromatic Bouquet
Rich Toffee, Tropical Passion Fruit & Sweet Vanilla

A lavish, sweet bouquet fills the room. Opulent butter toffee and vanilla fudge are accented by tropical fruit notes: grilled pineapple, passion fruit, and ripe plantain. A gentle thread of warm rum sweetness and fresh oak balances the aroma.

Butter Toffee Passion Fruit Grilled Pineapple Vanilla Fudge

Taste

Flavors & Finish
Rum-Soaked Raisins, Brown Sugar & Warm Lingering Spice

Mouth-filling and rounded with vanilla custard and sweet oak. Then the rum cask reveals itself with brown sugar, dark molasses, mango slices, and subtle cinnamon spice. The finish is remarkably warm, lingering, and luscious.

Brown Sugar Dark Molasses Warm Rum Finish Sweet Oak

The Craftsmanship & Tasting Narrative

The Balvenie distillery in Dufftown is one of the few distilleries in Scotland that still grows its own barley, operates a traditional floor maltings, maintains an on-site cooperage, and employs a resident coppersmith. This dedication to craft is evident across all their bottlings.

To craft Caribbean Cask, David Stewart created his own bespoke blend of West Indian rums, filled them into casks to season the oak, and once the wood was seasoned, replaced the rum with 14-year-old Balvenie whisky for several months of finishing. The result preserves the core honeyed Speyside identity while enriching it with tropical decadence.

Tasting Room Verdict

"An irresistibly indulgent dram. The rum influence is neither cloying nor heavy-handed; it acts as an exotic spice and caramel glaze over Balvenie's classic honeyed profile."
