Welcome to the Archive

Crafted For Those Who Savor Every Drop

Whiskey Archive is a dedicated journal celebrating the craft, heritage, and sensory poetry of distilled spirits. Every whiskey in our collection is evaluated neat in a tulip-shaped Glencairn glass, observing the natural spirit without ice or chill dilution.

We believe a great review must go beyond simple scores. Our sensory framework evaluates each pour across three cornerstone pillars:

1. Color
Cask wood extraction, natural hues, viscosity, and clinging glass tears.
2. Smell
Primary nose, aerated bouquet, esters, peat smoke, and baking spices.
3. Taste
Palate texture, mid-palate evolution, oak balance, and length of finish.

How We Conduct A Tasting

A simple four-step guide to uncovering the secrets inside your glass.

1. Choose The Glass
Always use a tulip-shaped Glencairn glass. The tapered rim concentrates delicate volatile aromas straight to your nose.
2. Inspect The Color
Hold the glass against a white surface or light. Swirl gently to observe the tears (legs) that trace viscosity and alcohol density.
3. Nose Gently
Part your lips slightly while inhaling with one nostril at a time. This prevents ethanol burn while revealing nuanced esters.
4. Sip & Savor
Take a small initial sip to coat your palate. On the second sip, evaluate front sweetness, mid-palate spices, and the length of the finish.
